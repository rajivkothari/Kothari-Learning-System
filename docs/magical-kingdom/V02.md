# Magical Kingdom V0.2 — engagement and wardrobe

This revision follows the first child playtest: a bridge with unused spaces was confusing, dress-up barely changed the character, and the activities offered too little replay. It keeps the two existing rooms and expands their play rather than adding more doors.

The first Ice bridge has exactly five sockets and five movable crystals. Replays become three bridges: a smaller construction, a repair with some stones already in place, and a larger construction. A repair answer counts only newly added crystals. The socket count always matches the complete bridge; the numeral and instruction name how many crystals to add. Moves remain reversible. Make magic is the only answer submission.

Garden replays contain three flowers and a broader eight-word picture vocabulary. The printed word remains a cue; this is beginning-letter practice, not a test of uncued phonemic awareness. All bridge activities also remain classified as cued practice. Longer sessions and decorative rewards do not change mastery policy.

The full-screen wardrobe has twenty choices across outfits, crowns, bows, wings, and wands. Four outfits change the whole costume: lavender, winter, garden, and starlight. A large preview shows the saved combination. Locked items show the mission goal; touching them cannot equip or unlock them. Successful assisted missions earn cosmetic gifts too. Gifts reward finished adventures, not independent mastery claims.

Unlocks derive from committed completed mission instances in the kingdom database, including earlier books in the supplied learner's family. No editable unlock flag or extra academic event is added. New adventure book preserves earned clothes, combinations, comfort settings, and prior evidence. Existing completed V0.1 missions count toward gifts when the game next opens. Unfinished original missions retain their original activities.

Sound effects now reuse five ElevenLabs recordings already present in the project's approved sound packs: placement, removal, success, retry, and discovery. The theme limits playback to one effect at a time, drops requests before the browser gesture gate, stops on mute/background/exit, and never requests a remote audio service during play. The adapter uses the installed expo-audio 57.0.5 types; the official SDK 57 audio reference was checked on 2026-10-09: https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/sdk/audio/.

**Spoken voice upgrade remains pending.** The current local speech fallback is still in use. `content/themes/magical-kingdom/narration-script.json` supplies 66 finite lines and the existing recorded project voice as a reference. The preparation script makes no service calls and spends no credits. An ElevenLabs connection is needed to generate, audition, bundle, and validate the new recordings. Reusing recorded effects does not establish equal spoken voice quality.

The new outfit PNGs were generated as edits of the invented original character and copied unchanged. Exact prompts and SHA-256 hashes are in `assets/themes/magical-kingdom/outfits/provenance.json`. They have transparent corners and a soft semitransparent glow around the figure. They remain prototype artwork pending human review. Accessories are authored vectors, and the character still uses a single greeting pose rather than a finished animation set.

Run `npm run web:export`, then `node scripts/serve-web.js dist-web --port 8093`; open `http://localhost:8093/?open=kingdom`. An existing server can keep serving the rebuilt export: refresh the page. Open Dress-up to explore the categories. Finish an Ice mission for winter clothing, a Garden mission for garden clothing, then replay both rooms to reach starlight clothing after four completed adventures. Choices persist across refresh and new books.

The implementation changes only kingdom-owned content, UI, director, and QA files. It reuses a pure browser audio gate and existing approved effect files from Elevator Quest without editing either. There is no shared engine, runtime, database schema, or Elevator Quest behavior change in this revision. Production still excludes this prototype through the existing Metro boundary.

See the V0.2 QA report and screenshot gallery under `qa/magical-kingdom-v02`. Browser checks do not establish performance, touch responsiveness, accessibility, interruptions, or audible quality on physical iPad or Fire tablets. A fresh offline browser installation is not supplied; offline gameplay is tested after the export's assets have loaded.
