# Magical Kingdom V0.3 — recorded narration

The kingdom now uses 67 bundled ElevenLabs recordings in place of platform speech. The connected workspace returned Jo M. ~ Friendly Professional Voice (`jemqINv7N9LKUclcLQnU`), matching the existing project's narration manifest. Generation uses `eleven_multilingual_v2`, one take per authored line, as selected by the owner. Four short audition takes were generated first. Returned generation prices total 2,755 credits / 55.10 US cents; this is the provider's reported generation cost, not an independently verified billing statement.

The pack covers the guide, dress-up greeting, both room introductions, help at each assistance stage, retries, celebrations, ten bridge counts, all authored repair counts, eight garden words, the gift announcement, and locked-item goals. Every clip has an exact script, generation/node/session identifiers, duration, byte count, and SHA-256 in `assets/themes/magical-kingdom/audio/manifest.json`. The 67 Scribe transcripts match after normalizing case, punctuation, and written number forms. No other lexical substitutions are accepted. `qa/magical-kingdom-voice-v1/transcripts.json` records both strings and the comparison result.

One player handles voice and effects. A new spoken line replaces the current clip; effects are dropped while narration plays. Requests before the browser gesture gate are dropped. Mute, backgrounding, room departure, and exit stop playback. Help narration now follows the actual clue, guided, or demonstration stage. Missing or broken audio stays optional: text and gestures still work, and no speech service is called during gameplay.

Native playback uses installed expo-audio 57.0.5. The official Expo SDK 57 reference was checked on 2026-10-09: https://docs.expo.dev/versions/v57.0.0/sdk/audio/. The browser adapter handles a rejected `HTMLMediaElement.play()` promise when fast interaction deliberately cancels a pending clip. It fetches only same-origin local export assets, retains voice blobs for offline play after loading, and revokes them on exit. A fresh offline browser installation is still not supplied.

The recordings were copied unchanged from the connector's download URLs. Build-time download/finalization scripts do not call generation APIs. The signed download staging files remain ignored under `.expo`; no credential or signed media URL is bundled in the game. `narration-script.json` stays the finite recording brief; regenerating the brief spends no credits.

Listening quality and physical tablet playback remain unreviewed. The voice identity and transcript checks establish what was generated and recognized, not whether a parent or child likes its tone or pace. The asset manifest therefore keeps `humanReviewed: false`. The prototype still has the existing two rooms; the shared learning engine, evidence policy, database schema, and Elevator Quest source are unchanged by this narration revision.

Refresh `http://localhost:8093/?open=kingdom`, turn Sound on in Comfort if needed, and use Hear it. The voice audition clip stays on the prototype branch (not integrated). See the voice QA report for executed checks and their limits.
